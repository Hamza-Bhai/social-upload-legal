Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Bollywood Fans Research ("the app") is a private research tool operated by a single individual for their own YouTube channel. It has no users other than its operator, no sign-up, and no public interface.

What data the app accesses

When connected to the operator's Google account, the app requests only read-only permissions:

  • YouTube Data API (read-only) the operator's own channel and video details, and public information about videos and channels.
  • YouTube Analytics API (read-only) view counts, watch time, subscriber changes and audience reports for the operator's own channel.

The app cannot upload, edit or delete videos, post comments, or change any channel setting, and it does not have permission to do so.

How the data is used

The data is used only to write a daily content plan for the operator's own channel: which topics are trending and which of the channel's own videos performed well. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

What data is stored, and where

  • OAuth tokens (access token and refresh token) are stored as encrypted secrets in the operator's private environment, used only to authenticate the app's own scheduled research runs.
  • Research notes summaries of public video statistics and the channel's own analytics are kept in the operator's private files.

No personal data belonging to any other person is collected, stored, or processed.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. Data is exchanged only with Google's APIs, as required to read it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the tool is in use and are deleted when access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party apps & services, which immediately ends the app's access to the account.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not usable by anyone other than its operator.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to bollywoodfans46@gmail.com.
