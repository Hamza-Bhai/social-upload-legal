Bollywood Fans Research

A personal content research tool for my own YouTube channel.

Bollywood Fans Research is a private, self-hosted script that helps me plan videos for my own YouTube channel, Bollywood Fans. Each day it looks at public YouTube data (trending videos and channels in my niche) and my own channel's analytics, and writes a content plan for me. It is not a product, not offered to other people, and has no sign-up.

It only reads data. It does not upload, edit, delete, comment, or change anything on YouTube.
