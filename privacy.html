Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Social Upload Automation ("the app") is a private automation tool operated by a single individual for their own social media accounts. It has no users other than its operator, no sign-up, and no public interface.

What data the app accesses

When connected to TikTok, the app requests only these permissions:

  • user.info.basic the account's open ID, display name and avatar, used to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • video.upload permission to upload a video to the account's TikTok inbox as a draft.

The app does not read the account's videos, followers, comments, messages, analytics, or any other data, and it does not have permission to do so.

What data is stored, and where

  • OAuth tokens (access token and refresh token) are stored as encrypted secrets in a private GitHub repository, used only to authenticate the app's own scheduled uploads.
  • Video and image files are the operator's own original content, stored in the operator's own cloud storage.
  • Upload records which piece of content was uploaded on which date are kept in the private repository so the same item is not uploaded twice.

No personal data belonging to any other person is collected, stored, or processed.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. Data is exchanged only with the platform APIs the content is being posted to (TikTok, and separately Meta and YouTube), as required to perform the upload.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the automation is in use and are deleted when access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions, which immediately and permanently ends the app's access to the account.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not usable by anyone other than its operator.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details listed on the operator's TikTok developer account.
