Social Upload Automation

A personal tool for scheduling my own social media content.

Social Upload Automation is a private, self-hosted script that publishes my own original short-form videos and images to my own social media accounts on a schedule. It is not a product, not offered to other people, and has no sign-up.

On TikTok specifically, it uses the Content Posting API in upload mode: videos are placed in my TikTok inbox as drafts, and I review and publish each one manually from the TikTok app.
